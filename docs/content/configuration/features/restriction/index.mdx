`features/restriction/settings.yml` holds the settings every kind of restriction shares. [Restriction](../../../admins/features/restriction/index.mdx) explains what restrictions do.

## settings.yml

<Setting
  name="enabled"
  takes="`true` or `false`"
  default="`true`"
  affects="whether restrictions can be used, for every provider that does not set its own value"
>

</Setting>

<Setting
  name="toggleTtl"
  takes="a positive duration"
  default='`"365d"`'
  affects="how long a restriction staff turned on lasts before it lifts itself"
>

A restriction lasts until staff lift it or this duration passes, whichever comes first. Without [replication](../../replication/index.mdx), a restart of the proxy lifts every restriction as well.

</Setting>

<Setting
  name="replication.toggleNamespace"
  takes="a non-blank string"
  default='`"identica:restriction:toggle"`'
  affects="the name under which proxies share their active restrictions"
  flag="advanced"
>

Proxies that should share their restrictions use the same value.

</Setting>

## Per provider

<Setting
  name="features.restriction.enabled"
  takes="`true` or `false`"
  default="absent"
  affects="whether restrictions can be used for this provider"
>

When absent, the shared `enabled` applies. `false` also turns off [join restriction](./join/index.mdx) for the provider.

</Setting>
