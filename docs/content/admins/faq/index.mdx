## General

<Accordions type="single">
  <Accordion title="Can I migrate data from another authentication plugin?">
    Identica ships no importer, but accounts can be registered from the console with their UUIDs, which a script can do for every account of the old plugin. See [Coming from another plugin](../guides/upgrading/from-another-plugin/index.mdx).

    If you would rather not write that script, ask in [Discord](https://discord.arcadeya.com): we may be able to provide one that you review and run against your own data.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Logging in

<Accordions type="single">
  <Accordion title="How do I stop players from moving or breaking blocks while they log in?">
    Identica is an identity plugin.
    It does not stop movement or block breaking while a player logs in.

    Send players who are logging in to a server where they cannot do either; see [Routing players](../guides/network/routing/index.mdx).
    We recommend [PicoLimbo](https://picolimbo.quozul.dev/about/introduction.html), because it can host virtual servers inside your proxy and does not require separate full backend servers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I stop players from using commands while they log in?">
    Block them on your proxy, or use an addon for that purpose; see [Keep players inside the login](../first-configuration/index.mdx).

    [Identica-CommandBlocker](https://modrinth.com/plugin/identica-commandblocker) can automatically block unrelated commands and allow only the ones needed while they log in.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I stop players from seeing announcements, staff chat, or other messages while they log in?">
    That should usually be handled outside Identica itself by the rest of your network setup.

    You can configure your other plugins so those messages are only sent to the servers where they belong, or use a community addon such as [Identica-ChatBlocker](https://modrinth.com/plugin/identica-chatblocker).
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
