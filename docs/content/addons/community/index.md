Community addons are plugins made by the community and not maintained by Identica developers.
They extend Identica without acting as providers themselves.

This page is the routing point for unofficial and community-maintained Identica addons.
Each addon is downloaded from where its author publishes it; report its issues to its author.

## Listed addons

Addons are listed alphabetically.

### Identica-ChatBlocker

Hides chat from players until they have authenticated, while Identica's own messages still reach them.

- Author: [purpurcof](https://github.com/purpurcof)
- Proxy: Velocity; BungeeCord untested
- Requires: [PacketEvents](https://modrinth.com/plugin/packetevents)
- Download: [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/plugin/identica-chatblocker)
- Source: [GitHub](https://github.com/purpurcof/Identica-ChatBlocker)

### Identica-CommandBlocker

Blocks commands until players have authenticated, except the ones Identica needs for logging in and those you allow.

- Author: [purpurcof](https://github.com/purpurcof)
- Proxy: Velocity; BungeeCord untested
- Download: [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/plugin/identica-commandblocker)
- Source: [GitHub](https://github.com/purpurcof/Identica-CommandBlocker)

## Add an addon

If you want a community addon to be listed here, request it through one of these channels:

- [Discord](https://discord.arcadeya.com)
- [GitHub Issues or Pull Requests](https://github.com/whereareiam/Identica)

Include what its entry shows:

- a one-sentence description of what it does
- its author
- the proxies it supports
- the plugins it requires, if any
- where it is downloaded and where its source is

Community addons can eventually be listed here as internal docs pages, external documentation links, or both.
