title: Official
description: Addons maintained by Identica's developers.
---

Official addons are maintained by Identica's developers and released together with Identica, each as a jar of its own. Install only the ones you want.

- [Dialogs](./dialogs/index.md): shows Identica's prompts, such as entering a password, as Minecraft dialogs instead
  of chat messages. Players whose client has no dialogs keep answering in chat.
