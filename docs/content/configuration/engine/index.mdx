---
title: engine.yml
description: Every setting of engine.yml.
---

`engine.yml` sets how each scenario runs: whether it asks the player or continues on its own, and how long a login that waits for the player is kept. [How logins work](../../admins/guides/logins/index.mdx) and [Journey modes](../../admins/guides/logins/journey-modes/index.mdx) explain them.

## behavior

Timers that apply to every scenario. The defaults suit every network; they only need to be longer than the slowest join.

<Setting
  name="bridgeTtl"
  takes="a positive duration"
  default='`"10m"`'
  affects="how long Identica keeps what it found out about a connecting player until their login starts, and a finished login until the player reaches their server"
  flag="advanced"
>

</Setting>

<Setting
  name="handshakeInstructionTtl"
  takes="a positive duration"
  default='`"10m"`'
  affects="how long a provider's decision to ask a connecting player for its own login, such as Premium's premium login, stays valid"
  flag="advanced"
>

</Setting>

## scenarios

`authentication`, `registration` and `migration` each have these settings. `registration` has `autoSelectSingleProvider` as well.

<Setting
  name="pipelineTtl"
  takes="a positive duration"
  default='`"5m"`'
  affects="how long a login that waits for the player is kept"
  flag="advanced"
>

It is also how long a pending move to another provider waits for the player's next join, and how long a username stays held for a connection that is logging in. See [Leaving during a login](../../admins/guides/logins/index.mdx#leaving-during-a-login).

</Setting>

<Setting
  name="allowResume"
  takes="`true` or `false`"
  default="`true`"
  affects="whether a player who leaves while logging in or registering continues where they stopped when they join again within `pipelineTtl`"
  flag="warning"
>

With `false`, such a player starts over. A reconnect the login asks for itself, such as the rejoin after choosing Premium, always continues. Whether a resumed login may pass an active join restriction is set in [join restriction settings](../features/restriction/join/index.mdx#resumebypassauthentication).

</Setting>

<Setting
  name="journeyMode"
  takes='`"SEAMLESS"` or `"INTERACTIVE"`'
  default='`"SEAMLESS"` for `authentication` and `registration`, `"INTERACTIVE"` for `migration`'
  affects="whether Identica asks the player, such as to choose a provider, or continues on its own as far as it can"
>

See [Journey modes](../../admins/guides/logins/journey-modes/index.mdx#modes).

</Setting>

<Setting
  name="journeyPolicy"
  takes='`"PREFER"` or `"STRICT"`'
  default='`"PREFER"`'
  affects="whether Identica may fall back to the other journey mode when the configured one cannot complete the login"
  flag="advanced"
>

With `SEAMLESS` and `STRICT`, a login that needs the player's input disconnects them with `engine.journey.step.interactionRequired`. See [Policies](../../admins/guides/logins/journey-modes/index.mdx#policies).

</Setting>

<Setting
  name="autoSelectSingleProvider"
  takes="`true` or `false`"
  default="`false`"
  affects="whether an `INTERACTIVE` registration with exactly one provider to choose from picks it without asking"
>

Only in `registration`.

</Setting>

<Tabs items={["YAML", "JSON"]}>
  <Tab value="YAML">
```yaml
behavior:
  bridgeTtl: "10m"
  handshakeInstructionTtl: "10m"
scenarios:
  authentication:
    pipelineTtl: "5m"
    allowResume: true
    journeyMode: "SEAMLESS"
    journeyPolicy: "PREFER"
  registration:
    pipelineTtl: "5m"
    allowResume: true
    journeyMode: "SEAMLESS"
    journeyPolicy: "PREFER"
    autoSelectSingleProvider: false
  migration:
    pipelineTtl: "5m"
    allowResume: true
    journeyMode: "INTERACTIVE"
    journeyPolicy: "PREFER"
```
  </Tab>
