---
title: Moving between providers
description: How a player moves their account to another provider, and why a move never lowers its protection.
---

A player can move their account from one provider to another, for example from Credential to Premium after buying the game. The account keeps its UUID and its data; only the way the player logs in changes.

## How a player moves

1. While logged in, the player types the new provider's command, such as `/premium` or `/credential`.
2. They confirm with `/premium confirm` or `/credential confirm`, or cancel with `cancel`.
3. Identica disconnects the player and keeps the move for the migration scenario's `pipelineTtl` in `engine.yml`, five minutes by default, for joins with the same username from the same address.
4. The player joins again and sets up the new provider, for example by logging in with their premium account or by choosing a password.

Staff can start, cancel and change moves with `/identica migration`; see [Managing accounts](../managing-accounts/index.mdx#providers-of-an-account).

The account may take its name from the new provider from then on, which can conflict with another account's name; the [username rules](../usernames/index.mdx) settle that.

## A move never lowers protection

A pending move continues only on a join that a provider verified:

- as the identity the account is already linked with, which is the provider being left. Identica then selects the new provider, and the player sets it up in the same session;
- or as the new provider's own identity, when the new provider verifies its logins itself, as Premium does.

Any other join that reaches Identica with a pending move cancels it, and the player reads `scenarios.migration.cancelled`. A join the provider being left refuses, such as an offline client using the name of a Premium account, changes nothing.

### From Premium to Credential

Premium asks every player who joins with the name of a Premium account to sign in with that premium account, so an offline client cannot take the name, and a pending move does not change that. The owner moves the account with `/credential` and `/credential confirm` while signed in. On the next join they sign in with the premium account once more, and that login lets them choose the password they use from then on.

### Leaving a provider that checks a password

A provider that proves ownership only with a prompt, such as Credential's password, cannot prove it again on the join after the confirmation. An account therefore leaves such a provider only for a provider that verifies its own logins, such as Premium.

Writing a provider that takes part in moves is covered in [Writing a provider](../../../../developers/extending/providers/index.mdx#moving-accounts).
