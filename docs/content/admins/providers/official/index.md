Official providers are released with Identica, on the same pages and with the same version.

- [Premium](./premium/index.md) lets players log in with their premium Minecraft account and keeps their account when they change their name at Mojang.
- [Credential](./credential/index.md) lets players log in with a password, for players without a premium account.

Both support every [feature](../../features/index.mdx).
