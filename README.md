**Identica** is an authentication and identity plugin for Velocity and BungeeCord in which every login method is a provider: passwords, premium accounts, and whatever your network needs next.

# Why Identica

- **Providers, not modes.** Credential and premium login are providers you can add, reorder, or remove.
- **Migration built in.** Players move between providers, even after a username change, without losing their account.
- **Verification.** Authenticator apps and other methods as a second factor.
- **Shared proxies.** Several proxies on one database, kept in sync through replication.
