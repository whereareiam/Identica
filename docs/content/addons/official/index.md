---
title: Official Addons
description: Addons maintained by Identica developers.
---

Official addons are plugins maintained by Identica developers that extend Identica without acting as providers.
Each one ships with Identica's releases as a jar of its own; install only the ones you want.

## Addons

- [Dialogs](./dialogs/index.md): shows Identica's prompts, such as entering a password, as Minecraft dialogs instead
  of chat messages. Players whose client has no dialogs keep answering in chat.
