---
title: Provider choice
description: Let Identica pick how a player logs in, or let the player choose.
---

A scenario can often get done in more than one way. Each enabled provider is one option, and a provider can offer several methods of its own, such as a quick check and a guided setup. `choice` in each scenario of `engine.yml` decides who picks among the options: Identica or the player.

| Scenario | Default |
| --- | --- |
| authentication | `AUTOMATIC` |
| registration | `AUTOMATIC` |
| migration | `AUTOMATIC` |

Whatever the choice, a player is never disconnected for having to type something. When the picked provider needs a password or an authenticator code, the player is asked for it.

## Choices

<Tabs items={["AUTOMATIC", "PROMPTED"]}>
  <Tab value="AUTOMATIC">
    Identica picks. The provider that [selection](../provider-selection/index.mdx) ranks first goes first: the one whose hostname the player joined through, the one linked to their account, then the one with the highest priority. A provider's methods follow their order. When an option cannot finish the login, the next one is tried. The player is only asked what the picked provider needs.

    This suits networks whose providers can tell players apart on their own. Its catch is the first join of an offline player with a premium username: the higher-priority Premium provider asks for a premium login right at the handshake, before the player sees anything. The client cannot complete it and is disconnected with an invalid-session message, which can look as if the server only accepts premium accounts. The next join from the same address within [`attemptTtl`](../../../../configuration/providers/index.mdx#attemptttl) continues with the next provider.

    Premium's [`CLIENT_PROFILE` detection](../../../providers/official/premium/detection/index.mdx) avoids this for clients on 1.19.1 or newer.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="PROMPTED">
    The player sees the options and picks one, by clicking it or with `/identica enroll <option>`. A new player who joins from an offline client with a premium username chooses the provider instead of being asked for a premium login first: the premium check at the handshake is skipped for a username without an account when the registration scenario is `PROMPTED`.

    The player is not asked when there is nothing to choose:

    - only one option is left, such as with one provider that can register them,
    - the player joined through a provider's [hostname](../provider-selection/index.mdx#hostnames), or their account is linked to a provider, and that provider has one option. With several, the player chooses only among its methods.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

```yaml
scenarios:
  registration:
    choice: "PROMPTED"
```

In a migration the administrator who starts it picks the provider, so `PROMPTED` only lets the player choose among that provider's methods.

## A provider's methods

Some providers offer several methods for a scenario; their own documentation names them. Each method is an option of its own: with `PROMPTED` the player sees it next to the provider's name and picks it as `<provider>:<method>`, such as `/identica enroll website:guided`.

`methods` in the provider's entry of `providers/providers.yml` leaves out methods or changes their order, per scenario:

```yaml
providers:
  - id: "website"
    methods:
      migration: ["guided", "quick"]
```

See [`methods`](../../../../configuration/providers/index.mdx#methods).

## What the player sees

The options are listed with `engine.journey.step.enrollment` from `messages.yml`. Its `entryFormat` names each option with `{optionName}` and the answer with `{optionId}`; `descriptions` may describe an option by its ID, such as `website:guided`, or every option of a provider by the provider's ID.

See [`engine.yml`](../../../../configuration/engine/index.mdx) for every scenario setting.
