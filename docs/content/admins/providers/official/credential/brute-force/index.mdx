---
title: Brute-force protection
description: Warn about and lock out players who keep typing wrong passwords.
---

Credential counts wrong passwords. After too many, it warns the player and then locks them out for a while. The settings are in `scenario.authentication.bruteforce` of `providers/Credential/settings.yml`:

```yaml
scenario:
  authentication:
    bruteforce:
      maxAttempts: 5
      lockout:
        enabled: true
        duration: "5m"
      warning:
        enabled: true
        thresholdPercentage: 50
```

- After `maxAttempts` wrong passwords within `lockout.duration`, the player is locked out for `lockout.duration`. `maxAttempts: 0` turns the protection off.
- With `lockout.enabled: false`, wrong passwords are still counted and warned about, but nobody is locked out.
- The warning starts once `thresholdPercentage` of the attempts are used: with 5 attempts and 50, from the third wrong password, which leaves two.

`lockout.duration` is also how long a wrong password is remembered, so with the defaults five wrong passwords within five minutes lock a player out.

The protection is part of the [sentinel](../../../../features/sentinel/index.mdx) feature: `features.sentinel.enabled: false` in Credential's entry of `providers/providers.yml` turns it off as well.

See [Settings](../settings/index.mdx#authenticationbruteforce) for every setting.
